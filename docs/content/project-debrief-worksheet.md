# Portfolio item review packet

Use this packet to reconstruct and reconsider each portfolio item without
repeating the interview that produced the current draft. The agent prepares the
packet from existing material. Bradley reviews the whole item at once, corrects
what is wrong, adds what is missing, and contributes ideas where useful.

The current draft is evidence, not authority. Mine it for facts, scenes, and
language, but do not inherit its outline or conclusions automatically.

## Portfolio contract

Primary audience: recruiters, hiring managers, and other people deciding whom
to consider for a role.

After a short visit, the portfolio should leave four beliefs:

- Bradley is competent. He has changed real operations and built working
  systems.
- Bradley is interesting. His software work comes out of businesses and
  problems he has lived with.
- Bradley is thoughtful. He can name the constraint, the tradeoff, and the
  boundary of a system.
- Bradley is clear. A reader can understand what he did, why it mattered, and
  what changed.

The portfolio does not nominate one project as the thing every visitor must
remember. Every included piece should be vivid, deeply known, and able to
reward attention. Different visitors may remember different work. The
cumulative impression of Bradley matters more than enforcing one shared
project takeaway.

The desired next action is quiet. A visitor should remember Bradley favorably,
keep him in consideration, or contact him when a relevant role appears. The
site should make contact easy without reading like an open-to-work campaign.

## How to use this

1. The agent reads the current draft and relevant repository material for one
   item.
2. The agent prefills every section below and labels statements as sourced
   fact, inference, gap, or question.
3. The agent presents the complete item packet and all questions together.
4. Bradley comments wherever he wants to correct, add, reject, or redirect.
   Unremarked sourced facts carry provisionally; inference never becomes fact
   through silence.
5. The agent revises the packet and records the confirmed version under
   `.context/portfolio-debriefs/`.
6. Only then does the session move to the next item.

Do not draft polished portfolio prose during this pass. The purpose is to
understand each item well enough that later writing has something real to say.

## Source labels

- `Fact` means the supplied material states it directly.
- `Inference` means the agent has interpreted or connected facts.
- `Gap` means the record cannot currently support a needed claim.
- `Question` means Bradley's answer would materially change the item.

Copy the item packet below once for each item.
